# Human publication checklist

## Six confirmations stored on each version

- Accuracy: each factual claim, diagram label, answer, and explanation checked; analogies do not teach misconceptions.
- Syllabus: objective mapped to the official Biology syllabus; no unsupported claims of full coverage or exam readiness.
- Humour: appropriate and understandable to the audience; nobody targeted or ridiculed.
- Accessibility: text stands alone; image descriptions, audio transcripts, video captions, readable labels checked.
- Rights: creator/source and permission/licence documented for every media asset.
- References: source independently checked; exact reference/page details recorded; AI suggestions not accepted blindly.

## Structure and media

- 1–3 consecutive cards and exactly three four-choice questions; one unambiguous answer each.
- Relevant content rather than unfilled media slots; remove UNREVIEWED labels only after review.
- Preview every asset on a suitable player/device; verify duration, dimensions, format, captions, transcript, and file size. Set metadata checked only after this work.
- Record review notes describing corrections and checks. Publication captures the actual acting staff reviewer and timestamp.

The software requires confirmations but cannot prove their truth. Do not tick them to bypass review. Test fixtures and seeded drafts are not approved educational samples.
