# Reusable AI drafting prompt

Use the official JAMB source and locator recorded in [syllabus mapping](syllabus-source.md). Do not substitute a third-party syllabus summary.

Use in an external AI tool for manual drafting; the app also supports admin-triggered text generation in AI-1. Replace bracketed inputs. Never provide student personal data.

---

Create an UNREVIEWED educational draft for Nigerian JAMB UTME Biology candidates.

Learning objective: [one objective checked against the official syllabus]
Syllabus reference and exact objective: [URL/section]
Verified teaching references supplied by reviewer: [sources/pages]
Format requested: [text, image storyboard, audio script, or video storyboard]

Produce one standalone funny post with exactly one content card, suitable for low-data use. Each card must contain an accurate plain-English explanation that stands alone without media. Add light, relatable humour; explicitly separate analogies from facts. Avoid stereotypes, ridicule, invented exam claims, and misleading simplifications.

For images, describe an original labelled diagram and alternative text. For audio, supply a script/transcript ≤90 seconds. For video, supply a storyboard/script ≤60 seconds, suggested timed captions, and text alternative. Do not claim media files have been generated, licensed, or validated.

If questions are requested, create exactly three with four distinct choices each; otherwise return no questions. Each requested question has one correct choice labelled a/b/c/d, and an explanation addressing the misconception. Do not invent past JAMB questions or claim these are official questions.

List claims requiring verification, possible misconceptions, humour risks, and suggested references. Mark references you have not verified. Do not state the content is reviewed, approved, or ready for automatic publication.

Output: title; objective; one card text and optional media script; questions/choices/correct letters/explanations; verification notes; source suggestions.

---

After generation: human checks scientific accuracy, syllabus mapping, humour, accessibility, rights, references, and media playback. Record AI tool/model/date and meaningful reviewer corrections. A convincing draft is not evidence of correctness.

## One-content-post update

New publications require one card. It can include text and one optional media attachment. Carousels are deferred. Keep humour and explanations understandable without reading another post first. Questions supply optional in-feed dares, not a compulsory quiz after the post.

Seed reruns preserve existing content, including older multi-card drafts. They do not convert or publish those drafts. For an older multi-card draft, a human must prepare one standalone card before publishing. For published material, use a reviewed replacement draft; never alter the original history.

## AI-1 text drafts and optional questions

Staff can now request a new text draft through admin, with supplied source excerpts and optional questions. Follow [AI setup](../ai-generation-setup.md). All review confirmations remain unset and publication is still an explicit human action. Posts can have zero or exactly three questions; only complete published quizzes supply dares. One or two questions block publication. Images/memes/videos still require manual preparation until their AI stages are separately approved.
