# JAMB Government and Chemistry sample posts

Run from backend:

~~~powershell
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe manage.py seed_jamb_subjects
~~~

Creates five Government and five Chemistry drafts, each with its own learning unit, version 1, and one text card. These posts have no questions or media; they do not supply dares. Reruns preserve existing posts and human edits. No API calls are made.

Government: separation of powers, checks and balances, constitution, rule of law, and federal versus unitary structures. Chemistry: filtration, equation balancing, the mole, gas pressure/Boyle's law, and physical versus chemical changes.

Topic mapping uses official [Government](https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/assets/uploads/Government.pdf) and [Chemistry](https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/assets/uploads/Chemistry.pdf) syllabus search results. Direct PDF retrieval returned the site shell; verify the current syllabus during review. These are original practice posts, not official JAMB questions.

In admin, open each new version, check the explanation and analogy, record checked teaching references and review notes, complete the six review confirmations, then publish explicitly. Adjust the objective if needed. Subjects appear on the phone only once they have published content. Select Government (JAMB) and Chemistry (JAMB), then refresh the feed online. Adding sample drafts does not approve expanding the invited pilot beyond Biology.
